▶How To Be Successful
如何成功
▶I've observed thousands of founders and thought a lot about what it takes to make a huge amount of money or to create something important.
我观察过成千上万的创始人，也认真想过：要赚到巨额财富、或者创造出重要的东西，到底需要什么。
  • 词observe /əbˈzɜːv/ v. 观察；注意到。名词 observation。写作里可替换 notice / see。
  • 词founder /ˈfaʊndə/ n. 创始人。found v. 创立（注意不是 find 的过去式）。
  • 搭配what it takes to do sth 做成某事需要具备什么（条件/素质）。例：Do you have what it takes to be a leader?
▶Usually, people start off wanting the former and end up wanting the latter.
通常，人们一开始想要的是前者（钱），最后想要的却是后者（做出重要的东西）。
  • 搭配start off doing 以……开始 ↔ end up doing 最终（落得）……，这对搭配常成对出现。
  • 搭配the former … the latter 前者……后者。雅思写作中指代两者的高分表达。
▶Here are 13 thoughts about how to achieve such outlier success.
下面是我关于如何取得这种"极端少数"式成功的 13 条想法。
  • 词outlier /ˈaʊtlaɪə/ n. 异常值；局外者；远超常态的人或事。outlier success = 远超常人、统计意义上极少见的成功。
▶Everything here is easier to do once you've already reached a baseline degree of success (through privilege or effort) and want to put in the work to turn that into outlier success.
如果你已经（靠出身优势或自身努力）达到了一个基础水平的成功，并且愿意投入功夫把它变成极端的成功，那么这里说的一切都会更容易做到。
  • 词baseline /ˈbeɪslaɪn/ n./adj. 基线；基准水平。
  • 词privilege /ˈprɪvəlɪdʒ/ n. 特权；优越条件（这里指出身好、起点高）。高频话题词：social privilege。
  • 搭配put in the work / effort 投入功夫、下功夫。
  • 搭配turn A into B 把 A 变成 B。
▶But much of it applies to anyone.
但其中大部分内容适用于任何人。
  • 搭配apply to 适用于。区分 apply for 申请。

1. Compound yourself 让自己复利增长

▶Compounding is magic.
复利就是魔法。
▶Look for it everywhere.
到处去寻找它。
▶Exponential curves are the key to wealth generation.
指数曲线是创造财富的关键。
  • 词exponential /ˌekspəˈnenʃl/ adj. 指数的；急剧增长的。exponential growth 指数式增长（写作描述趋势常用）。
  • 搭配the key to sth ……的关键（to 是介词，后接名词/动名词）。
  • 词generation n. 产生；一代人。wealth generation 财富创造。
▶A medium-sized business that grows 50% in value every year becomes huge in a very short amount of time.
一家中等规模的企业，如果每年价值增长 50%，会在很短时间内变得非常庞大。
  • 搭配grow (by) X% in value 价值增长 X%。
▶Few businesses in the world have true network effects and extreme scalability.
世界上很少有企业真正具备网络效应和极强的可扩展性。
  • 搭配network effect(s) 网络效应（用户越多，产品对每个用户越有价值）。
  • 词scalability /ˌskeɪləˈbɪləti/ n. 可扩展性；可规模化。scale v. 扩大规模；scalable adj.
▶But with technology, more and more will.
但借助技术，会有越来越多的企业具备这些特质。
▶It's worth a lot of effort to find them and create them.
为了找到和创造这样的企业，花大力气是值得的。
  • 搭配be worth (doing / sth) 值得……。It's worth a lot of effort to do = 值得花大力气去做。
▶You also want to be an exponential curve yourself—you should aim for your life to follow an ever-increasing up-and-to-the-right trajectory.
你自己也要成为一条指数曲线——你应该让自己的人生沿着一条不断上升、向右上方延伸的轨迹前进。
  • 搭配aim for sth / aim to do 力求、以……为目标。aim for your life to follow… = 让人生朝……方向走。
  • 搭配ever-increasing 不断增长的（ever- + 现在分词 = 持续地……，如 ever-changing 日新月异的）。
  • 搭配up and to the right 向右上方（形容图表一路走高的曲线，商业口语）。
  • 词trajectory /trəˈdʒektəri/ n. 轨迹；发展轨道。career trajectory 职业轨迹。
▶It's important to move towards a career that has a compounding effect—most careers progress fairly linearly.
重要的是选择一份具有复利效应的职业——大多数职业都只是相当线性地发展。
  • 词progress /prəˈɡres/ v. 进展 /ˈprəʊɡres/ n. 进步（动名词重音不同）。
  • 词linearly /ˈlɪniəli/ adv. 线性地。linear adj. 线性的、直线式的。
▶You don't want to be in a career where people who have been doing it for two years can be as effective as people who have been doing it for twenty—your rate of learning should always be high.
你不会想待在这样一种职业里：干了两年的人和干了二十年的人效率一样——你的学习速度应该始终保持在高位。
  • 搭配as effective as 和……一样有效（as + adj. + as 同级比较）。
  • 搭配rate of learning 学习速度。
▶As your career progresses, each unit of work you do should generate more and more results.
随着职业发展，你投入的每一单位工作都应该产出越来越多的成果。
  • 词generate /ˈdʒenəreɪt/ v. 产生、创造。雅思写作替换 produce / create / bring about。
▶There are many ways to get this leverage, such as capital, technology, brand, network effects, and managing people.
获得这种杠杆的方法有很多，比如资本、技术、品牌、网络效应以及管理他人。
  • 词leverage /ˈliːvərɪdʒ/ n. 杠杆作用；影响力 v. 利用。本文核心词：用较少投入撬动更大产出。
  • 词capital /ˈkæpɪtl/ n. 资本；首都；大写字母。
▶It's useful to focus on adding another zero to whatever you define as your success metric—money, status, impact on the world, or whatever.
专注于让你定义的成功指标再多一个零，是很有用的——不管这个指标是金钱、地位、对世界的影响力还是别的什么。
  • 搭配add another zero to 在……后面再加一个零，即"提升一个数量级、翻十倍"。
  • 搭配define A as B 把 A 定义为 B。
  • 词metric /ˈmetrɪk/ n. 衡量标准；指标。
  • 词status /ˈsteɪtəs/ n. 地位；状态。social status 社会地位。
  • 搭配impact on 对……的影响（impact 后接介词 on）。
▶I am willing to take as much time as needed between projects to find my next thing.
在两个项目之间，我愿意花尽可能多的时间去寻找下一件要做的事。
  • 搭配be willing to do 愿意做。
  • 搭配as much … as needed 需要多少就……多少。
▶But I always want it to be a project that, if successful, will make the rest of my career look like a footnote.
但我总希望它是这样一个项目：一旦成功，会让我其余的职业生涯都显得像个脚注。
  • 词footnote /ˈfʊtnəʊt/ n. 脚注；次要的事。look like a footnote = 显得微不足道。
▶Most people get bogged down in linear opportunities.
大多数人陷在线性增长的机会里出不来。
  • 搭配get bogged down (in) 陷入困境、被……拖住而停滞不前。bog n. 沼泽。
▶Be willing to let small opportunities go to focus on potential step changes.
要舍得放掉小机会，去专注于可能带来跃迁式变化的事情。
  • 搭配let sth go 放手、放弃。
  • 搭配step change 跃升、质的飞跃（不是渐进的改变，而是一下跳到新台阶）。
  • 词potential /pəˈtenʃl/ adj. 潜在的 n. 潜力。
▶I think the biggest competitive advantage in business—either for a company or for an individual's career—is long-term thinking with a broad view of how different systems in the world are going to come together.
我认为商业中最大的竞争优势——不论对一家公司还是对个人职业而言——是长期思维，并且对世界上各种不同系统将如何汇聚到一起有宏观的视野。
  • 搭配competitive advantage 竞争优势。
  • 搭配long-term thinking 长期思维。
  • 搭配a broad view of 对……的宏观视野。
  • 搭配come together 汇聚、结合在一起。
▶One of the notable aspects of compound growth is that the furthest out years are the most important.
复利增长一个值得注意的特点是：最远的那些年份才是最重要的。
  • 词notable /ˈnəʊtəbl/ adj. 值得注意的；显著的。
  • 词aspect /ˈæspekt/ n. 方面。
  • 搭配the furthest out years 最遥远的年份（复利后期增长最猛）。
▶In a world where almost no one takes a truly long-term view, the market richly rewards those who do.
在一个几乎没有人真正着眼长远的世界里，市场会丰厚地回报那些这样做的人。
  • 搭配take a long-term view 着眼长远、从长计议。
  • 词richly adv. 丰厚地；充分地。richly reward 丰厚回报。
▶Trust the exponential, be patient, and be pleasantly surprised.
相信指数增长，保持耐心，然后等着惊喜到来。
  • 搭配be pleasantly surprised 感到惊喜。

2. Have almost too much self-belief 要有近乎过度的自信

▶Self-belief is immensely powerful.
自信的力量极其强大。
  • 词self-belief n. 自信（= self-confidence）。
  • 词immensely /ɪˈmensli/ adv. 极其、非常。immense adj. 巨大的。
▶The most successful people I know believe in themselves almost to the point of delusion.
我认识的最成功的人，对自己的相信几乎到了妄想的地步。
  • 搭配believe in sb 信任某人、相信某人的能力（区分 believe sb 相信某人说的话）。
  • 搭配to the point of 到了……的程度。
  • 词delusion /dɪˈluːʒn/ n. 错觉；妄想。delude v. 欺骗。
▶Cultivate this early.
尽早培养这种自信。
  • 词cultivate /ˈkʌltɪveɪt/ v. 培养；耕种。雅思写作高频：cultivate a habit / skill / interest。
▶As you get more data points that your judgment is good and you can consistently deliver results, trust yourself more.
当你积累了更多证据表明你的判断是对的、你能稳定地交出成果时，就更多地相信自己。
  • 搭配data point 数据点；（引申）证据。
  • 词judgment /ˈdʒʌdʒmənt/ n. 判断（力）。
  • 词consistently /kənˈsɪstəntli/ adv. 一贯地、稳定地。
  • 搭配deliver results 交付成果、做出成绩（职场常用）。
▶If you don't believe in yourself, it's hard to let yourself have contrarian ideas about the future.
如果你不相信自己，就很难允许自己对未来抱有与众不同的看法。
  • 词contrarian /kənˈtreəriən/ adj./n. 反主流的（人）；逆向思维的。contrary adj. 相反的。
▶But this is where most value gets created.
但大部分价值恰恰是在这里被创造出来的。
▶I remember when Elon Musk took me on a tour of the SpaceX factory many years ago.
我记得很多年前，埃隆·马斯克带我参观 SpaceX 的工厂。
  • 搭配take sb on a tour of 带某人参观……。
▶He talked in detail about manufacturing every part of the rocket, but the thing that sticks in memory was the look of absolute certainty on his face when he talked about sending large rockets to Mars.
他详细讲了火箭每个零件的制造，但让我记忆犹新的，是他谈到把大型火箭送上火星时脸上那种绝对笃定的神情。
  • 搭配in detail 详细地。
  • 词manufacture /ˌmænjuˈfæktʃə/ v./n. 制造。
  • 搭配stick in (one's) memory / mind 留在记忆中、令人难忘。
  • 词certainty /ˈsɜːtnti/ n. 确定、把握。
  • 搭配the look on one's face 某人脸上的神情。
▶I left thinking "huh, so that's the benchmark for what conviction looks like."
我离开时心想："哈，原来这就是'坚信'该有的标杆样子。"
  • 词benchmark /ˈbentʃmɑːk/ n. 基准；标杆。本文高频词，可用于写作 set a benchmark for。
  • 词conviction /kənˈvɪkʃn/ n. 坚定的信念；（法律）定罪。convince v. 说服。
▶Managing your own morale—and your team's morale—is one of the greatest challenges of most endeavors.
管理好自己的士气——以及团队的士气——是大多数事业中最大的挑战之一。
  • 词morale /məˈrɑːl/ n. 士气（注意区分 moral /ˈmɒrəl/ 道德的）。
  • 词endeavor /ɪnˈdevə/ n. 努力；事业（英式拼写 endeavour）。
▶It's almost impossible without a lot of self-belief.
没有强大的自信，这几乎不可能做到。
▶And unfortunately, the more ambitious you are, the more the world will try to tear you down.
而遗憾的是，你越有雄心，这个世界就越想把你打垮。
  • 搭配the more …, the more … 越……就越……（写作常用比较句型）。
  • 词ambitious /æmˈbɪʃəs/ adj. 有雄心的。ambition n.
  • 搭配tear sb down 打击、贬低、把某人搞垮。
▶Most highly successful people have been really right about the future at least once at a time when people thought they were wrong.
大多数非常成功的人，都至少有一次在别人都认为他们错了的时候，对未来做出了真正正确的判断。
  • 搭配at a time when 在……的时候。
▶If not, they would have faced much more competition.
否则，他们早就会面对多得多的竞争。
  • 语法if not + would have done：虚拟语气，表示与过去事实相反。
▶Self-belief must be balanced with self-awareness.
自信必须与自我认知相平衡。
  • 搭配be balanced with 与……相平衡。
  • 词self-awareness n. 自我认知、自知之明。
▶I used to hate criticism of any sort and actively avoided it.
我过去讨厌任何形式的批评，并且主动回避它。
  • 搭配used to do 过去常常（现在不了）。
  • 搭配of any sort 任何形式的（= of any kind）。
  • 词criticism /ˈkrɪtɪsɪzəm/ n. 批评。criticize v.
▶Now I try to always listen to it with the assumption that it's true, and then decide if I want to act on it or not.
现在我总是尽量先假设批评是对的去听，然后再决定要不要据此行动。
  • 搭配with the assumption that 假定……。assumption /əˈsʌmpʃn/ n. 假设。
  • 搭配act on sth 根据（建议、信息）采取行动。
▶Truth-seeking is hard and often painful, but it is what separates self-belief from self-delusion.
追求真相很难，也常常令人痛苦，但正是它把自信和自欺区分开来。
  • 搭配separate A from B 把 A 与 B 区分开。
  • 词self-delusion n. 自欺。
▶This balance also helps you avoid coming across as entitled and out of touch.
这种平衡也能帮你避免给人一种自以为是、脱离现实的印象。
  • 搭配avoid doing 避免做（avoid 后接动名词）。
  • 搭配come across as 给人……的印象。
  • 词entitled /ɪnˈtaɪtld/ adj. 自以为理应享有特权的；有资格的。
  • 搭配out of touch 脱节、不了解实际情况。

3. Learn to think independently 学会独立思考

▶Entrepreneurship is very difficult to teach because original thinking is very difficult to teach.
创业很难教，因为原创性思维很难教。
  • 词entrepreneurship /ˌɒntrəprəˈnɜːʃɪp/ n. 创业；企业家精神。entrepreneur n. 企业家、创业者。
  • 词original adj. 原创的；最初的。
▶School is not set up to teach this—in fact, it generally rewards the opposite.
学校的设置本就不是为了教这个——事实上，学校通常奖励的恰恰是相反的东西。
  • 搭配be set up to do 被设计/建立起来去做……。
  • 搭配the opposite 相反的事物。
▶So you have to cultivate it on your own.
所以你只能自己去培养。
  • 搭配on your own 独自、靠自己。
▶Thinking from first principles and trying to generate new ideas is fun, and finding people to exchange them with is a great way to get better at this.
从第一性原理出发思考、尝试产生新想法是很有趣的事；找到能和你交流这些想法的人，是提升这项能力的好办法。
  • 搭配first principles 第一性原理（回到最基本的事实重新推理，而非类比照搬）。
  • 搭配exchange ideas with sb 与某人交流想法。
  • 搭配get better at 在……方面变得更好。
▶The next step is to find easy, fast ways to test these ideas in the real world.
下一步，是找到简单、快速的方法在现实世界中检验这些想法。
▶"I will fail many times, and I will be really right once" is the entrepreneurs' way.
"我会失败很多次，但会有一次真正做对"——这就是创业者的方式。
▶You have to give yourself a lot of chances to get lucky.
你得给自己很多次走运的机会。
▶One of the most powerful lessons to learn is that you can figure out what to do in situations that seem to have no solution.
最有力量的一课是：即使在看似无解的处境中，你也能想出该怎么办。
  • 搭配figure out 弄明白、想出（办法）。口语和写作都高频，正式替换 work out。
▶The more times you do this, the more you will believe it.
你这样做的次数越多，就会越相信这一点。
▶Grit comes from learning you can get back up after you get knocked down.
坚毅来自于这样的认识：被击倒之后，你还能重新站起来。
  • 词grit /ɡrɪt/ n. 坚毅、韧性；沙砾。
  • 搭配get back up 重新站起来。
  • 搭配get knocked down 被击倒。

4. Get good at "sales" 擅长"销售"

▶Self-belief alone is not sufficient—you also have to be able to convince other people of what you believe.
光有自信是不够的——你还必须能说服别人相信你所相信的东西。
  • 词sufficient /səˈfɪʃnt/ adj. 足够的（= enough，更正式）。
  • 搭配convince sb of sth 使某人相信某事。
▶All great careers, to some degree, become sales jobs.
所有伟大的职业，在某种程度上都会变成销售工作。
  • 搭配to some degree / to some extent 在某种程度上（写作让步常用）。
▶You have to evangelize your plans to customers, prospective employees, the press, investors, etc.
你必须向客户、潜在员工、媒体、投资人等热情宣传你的计划。
  • 词evangelize /ɪˈvændʒəlaɪz/ v. 传教；热情宣传、布道。
  • 词prospective /prəˈspektɪv/ adj. 潜在的、未来的。prospective students 准学生。
  • 搭配the press 新闻界、媒体。
▶This requires an inspiring vision, strong communication skills, some degree of charisma, and evidence of execution ability.
这需要一个鼓舞人心的愿景、很强的沟通能力、一定的个人魅力，以及证明你执行力的证据。
  • 词inspiring adj. 鼓舞人心的。inspire v.

要有意志力，就得乐观——希望这是一种可以通过练习提升的性格特质。
  • 词optimistic /ˌɒptɪˈmɪstɪk/ adj. 乐观的 ↔ pessimistic /ˌpesɪˈmɪstɪk/ 悲观的。
  • 搭配personality trait 性格特质。trait /treɪt/ n. 特征。
▶I have never met a very successful pessimistic person.
我从没见过一个非常成功的悲观主义者。

10. Be hard to compete with 让别人难以和你竞争

▶Most people understand that companies are more valuable if they are difficult to compete with.
大多数人都明白，公司越难被竞争，就越有价值。
▶This is important, and obviously true.
这很重要，也显而易见。
▶But this holds true for you as an individual as well.
但这对你个人来说同样成立。
  • 搭配hold true (for) （对……）适用、成立。
▶If what you do can be done by someone else, it eventually will be, and for less money.
如果你做的事别人也能做，那它最终会被别人做掉，而且花的钱更少。
  • 词eventually /ɪˈventʃuəli/ adv. 最终。
▶The best way to become difficult to compete with is to build up leverage.
让自己难以被竞争的最好办法，是积累杠杆。
  • 搭配build up 逐步积累、建立。
▶For example, you can do it with personal relationships, by building a strong personal brand, or by getting good at the intersection of multiple different fields.
比如，你可以靠人际关系，靠打造强大的个人品牌，或者靠精通多个不同领域的交叉地带来做到。
  • 搭配personal brand 个人品牌。
  • 词intersection /ˌɪntəˈsekʃn/ n. 交叉点；十字路口。the intersection of A and B A 与 B 的交叉领域。
▶There are many other strategies, but you have to figure out some way to do it.
还有很多其他策略，但你必须想出某种办法做到这一点。
▶Most people do whatever most people they hang out with do.
大多数人都在做他们身边大多数人在做的事。
  • 搭配hang out with 和……一起玩、待在一起。
▶This mimetic behavior is usually a mistake—if you're doing the same thing everyone else is doing, you will not be hard to compete with.
这种模仿行为通常是个错误——如果你做的和别人都一样，那你就不会难以被竞争。
  • 词mimetic /mɪˈmetɪk/ adj. 模仿的（源自哲学家基拉尔的"模仿欲望"理论）。mimic v. 模仿。

11. Build a network 建立人脉网络

▶Great work requires teams.
伟大的工作需要团队。
▶Developing a network of talented people to work with—sometimes closely, sometimes loosely—is an essential part of a great career.
建立一个由有才华的人组成、可以一起合作的人脉网络——有时紧密、有时松散——是伟大职业生涯中必不可少的一部分。
  • 词essential /ɪˈsenʃl/ adj. 必不可少的、极其重要的。
▶The size of the network of really talented people you know often becomes the limiter for what you can accomplish.
你认识的真正有才华的人的数量，往往会成为你能取得多大成就的上限。
  • 词limiter n. 限制因素、上限。
  • 词accomplish /əˈkʌmplɪʃ/ v. 完成、实现。
▶An effective way to build a network is to help people as much as you can.
建立人脉的一个有效方法，是尽你所能地帮助别人。
  • 搭配as much as you can 尽你所能。
▶Doing this, over a long period of time, is what lead to most of my best career opportunities and three of my four best investments.
长期坚持这样做，带来了我大部分最好的职业机会，以及我四笔最佳投资中的三笔。
  • 搭配over a long period of time 在很长一段时间里。
  • 注（注：原文 lead 应为 led，是作者笔误。）
▶I'm continually surprised how often something good happens to me because of something I did to help a founder ten years ago.
我一再惊讶地发现，常常因为十年前我帮过某位创始人的一件事，而有好事降临到我身上。
  • 词continually adv. 不断地、一再地。
▶One of the best ways to build a network is to develop a reputation for really taking care of the people who work with you.
建立人脉最好的方法之一，是树立"真正照顾与你共事的人"的名声。
  • 搭配develop / have a reputation for 以……著称、享有……的名声。
  • 搭配take care of 照顾。
▶Be overly generous with sharing the upside; it will come back to you 10x.
分享收益时要格外慷慨；它会十倍地回报你。
  • 词overly adv. 过度地。
  • 搭配be generous with 在……方面慷慨。
  • 词upside n. 好处、收益（↔ downside 坏处）。
  • 搭配come back to you 回报到你身上。
▶Also, learn how to evaluate what people are great at, and put them in those roles.
另外，要学会评估别人擅长什么，并把他们放到相应的岗位上。
  • 词evaluate /ɪˈvæljueɪt/ v. 评估。evaluation n.
▶(This is the most important thing I have learned about management, and I haven't read much about it.)
（这是我在管理上学到的最重要的一点，而我很少读到相关的论述。）
▶You want to have a reputation for pushing people hard enough that they accomplish more than they thought they could, but not so hard they burn out.
你希望拥有这样的名声：对人要求足够严格，让他们取得超出自己预期的成就，但又不至于把他们压垮。
  • 搭配push sb (hard) 督促、逼某人（努力）。
  • 搭配so … (that) 如此……以至于。
▶Everyone is better at some things than others.
每个人都有比别的方面更擅长的事情。
▶Define yourself by your strengths, not your weaknesses.
用你的长处而不是短处来定义自己。
  • 搭配define oneself by 用……来定义自己。
  • 词strength n. 长处；力量 ↔ weakness n. 弱点。
▶Acknowledge your weaknesses and figure out how to work around them, but don't let them stop you from doing what you want to do.
承认你的弱点，想办法绕开它们，但别让它们阻止你去做你想做的事。
  • 词acknowledge /əkˈnɒlɪdʒ/ v. 承认；致谢。
  • 搭配work around 绕过（问题）、变通解决。
  • 搭配stop sb from doing 阻止某人做某事。
▶"I can't do X because I'm not good at Y" is something I hear from entrepreneurs surprisingly often, and almost always reflects a lack of creativity.
"我做不了 X，因为我不擅长 Y"——我从创业者那里听到这句话的频率高得惊人，而这几乎总是反映出创造力的缺乏。
  • 词reflect v. 反映；反思。
  • 搭配a lack of 缺乏。
  • 词creativity /ˌkriːeɪˈtɪvəti/ n. 创造力。
▶The best way to make up for your weaknesses is to hire complementary team members instead of just hiring people who are good at the same things you are.
弥补弱点的最好办法，是招募能和你互补的团队成员，而不是只招和你擅长同样事情的人。
  • 搭配make up for 弥补。
  • 词complementary /ˌkɒmplɪˈmentri/ adj. 互补的。注意区分 complimentary 赞美的；免费的。
  • 搭配instead of 而不是。
▶A particularly valuable part of building a network is to get good at discovering undiscovered talent.
建立人脉中特别有价值的一环，是学会发掘尚未被发现的人才。
  • 词undiscovered adj. 未被发现的。
  • 词talent n. 才能；人才（集合名词）。
▶Quickly spotting intelligence, drive, and creativity gets much easier with practice.
快速识别一个人的聪明、干劲和创造力，会随着练习变得容易得多。
  • 词spot v. 发现、认出 n. 地点；斑点。
  • 词intelligence n. 智力、聪明才智；情报。
▶The easiest way to learn is just to meet a lot of people, and keep track of who goes on to impress you and who doesn't.
最简单的学习方法就是多见人，并持续记录谁后来让你刮目相看、谁没有。
  • 搭配keep track of 记录、跟踪了解。
  • 词impress v. 给……留下深刻印象。
▶Remember that you are mostly looking for rate of improvement, and don't overvalue experience or current accomplishment.
记住，你主要看的是一个人的进步速度，不要高估经验或当前的成就。
  • 搭配look for 寻找。
  • 搭配rate of improvement 进步速度。
  • 词overvalue v. 高估……的价值。
▶I try to always ask myself when I meet someone new "is this person a force of nature?"
每次遇到新的人，我都会问自己："这个人是不是一股不可阻挡的自然力量？"
  • 搭配a force of nature 自然之力；（形容人）精力充沛、势不可挡、极具影响力的人。
▶It's a pretty good heuristic for finding people who are likely to accomplish great things.
这是一个相当好用的经验法则，用来找出那些可能成就大事的人。
  • 词heuristic /hjʊˈrɪstɪk/ n. 启发法、经验法则 adj. 启发式的。
  • 搭配be likely to do 很可能做……（写作中表达可能性的高频句型）。
▶A special case of developing a network is finding someone eminent to take a bet on you, ideally early in your career.
建立人脉中有一种特殊情况：找到一位德高望重的人愿意押注于你，最好是在职业生涯早期。
  • 词eminent /ˈemɪnənt/ adj. 杰出的、知名的、德高望重的。
  • 搭配take a bet on sb 押注某人、对某人下注（看好并支持）。
  • 词ideally adv. 理想情况下、最好是。
▶The best way to do this, no surprise, is to go out of your way to be helpful.
做到这一点的最好办法，毫不意外，就是不遗余力地帮助别人。
  • 搭配no surprise 毫不意外（插入语）。
  • 搭配go out of one's way to do 特地、不怕麻烦地去做某事。
▶(And remember that you have to pay this forward at some point later!)
（并且记住，你之后某个时候也要把这份善意传递下去！）
  • 搭配pay it forward 把（受到的）善意传递给下一个人。区分 pay back 报答施恩者本人。
  • 搭配at some point 在某个时候。
▶Finally, remember to spend your time with positive people who support your ambitions.
最后，记得和支持你抱负的积极的人待在一起。
  • 词ambition /æmˈbɪʃn/ n. 抱负、雄心。

12. You get rich by owning things 靠拥有东西致富

▶The biggest economic misunderstanding of my childhood was that people got rich from high salaries.
我童年时对经济最大的误解，是以为人们靠高薪致富。
  • 词misunderstanding n. 误解。
  • 词salary n. 薪水。
▶Though there are some exceptions—entertainers for example —almost no one in the history of the Forbes list has gotten there with a salary.
虽然也有一些例外——比如娱乐明星——但在福布斯富豪榜的历史上，几乎没有人是靠工资上榜的。
  • 词exception /ɪkˈsepʃn/ n. 例外。with the exception of 除……之外。
  • 词entertainer n. 艺人、演艺人员。
▶You get truly rich by owning things that increase rapidly in value.
真正让你致富的，是拥有那些价值快速上涨的东西。
  • 搭配increase in value 增值。
  • 词rapidly adv. 迅速地。
▶This can be a piece of a business, real estate, natural resource, intellectual property, or other similar things.
这可以是一家企业的一部分股份、房地产、自然资源、知识产权或其他类似的东西。
  • 搭配real estate 房地产、不动产。
  • 搭配natural resource(s) 自然资源。
  • 搭配intellectual property (IP) 知识产权。
▶But somehow or other, you need to own equity in something, instead of just selling your time.
但无论如何，你都需要拥有某样东西的股权，而不仅仅是出卖你的时间。
  • 搭配somehow or other 不知怎么地、无论如何总得。
  • 词equity /ˈekwəti/ n. 股权、股本；公平。own equity in 持有……的股份。
▶Time only scales linearly.
时间只能线性增长。
▶The best way to make things that increase rapidly in value is by making things people want at scale.
打造快速增值的东西，最好的办法是大规模地做出人们想要的东西。
  • 搭配at scale 大规模地。
  • 搭配make things people want 做人们想要的东西（YC 的经典口号）。

13. Be internally driven 由内在驱动

▶Most people are primarily externally driven; they do what they do because they want to impress other people.
大多数人主要是受外在驱动的：他们做事是因为想给别人留下好印象。
  • 词primarily /praɪˈmerəli/ adv. 主要地。
  • 搭配externally driven ↔ internally driven 外在驱动 ↔ 内在驱动。
▶This is bad for many reasons, but here are two important ones.
出于很多原因，这都不好，这里说两个重要的。
▶First, you will work on consensus ideas and on consensus career tracks.
第一，你会去做大家都认同的想法，走大家都认同的职业路径。
  • 词consensus /kənˈsensəs/ n. 共识。reach a consensus 达成共识。consensus ideas 主流共识型想法。
  • 搭配career track 职业路径。
▶You will care a lot—much more than you realize—if other people think you're doing the right thing.
你会非常在意——比你意识到的还要在意——别人是否认为你在做正确的事。
▶This will probably prevent you from doing truly interesting work, and even if you do, someone else would have done it anyway.
这很可能阻碍你去做真正有意思的工作；即便你做了，别人反正也会做出来。
  • 搭配anyway 无论如何、反正。
▶Second, you will usually get risk calculations wrong.
第二，你通常会算错风险。
  • 搭配get sth wrong 把……弄错。
  • 词calculation n. 计算、估算。
▶You'll be very focused on keeping up with other people and not falling behind in competitive games, even in the short term.
你会非常专注于跟上别人、不在竞争游戏中落后，哪怕只是在短期内。
  • 搭配keep up with 跟上。
  • 搭配fall behind 落后。
  • 搭配in the short term ↔ in the long term 短期内 ↔ 长期来看。
▶Smart people seem to be especially at risk of such externally-driven behavior.
聪明人似乎尤其容易陷入这种受外在驱动的行为。
  • 搭配be at risk of 有……的风险。
▶Being aware of it helps, but only a little—you will likely have to work super-hard to not fall in the mimetic trap.
意识到这一点有帮助，但帮助不大——你很可能需要付出极大的努力，才能不掉进模仿的陷阱。
  • 搭配be aware of 意识到。
  • 搭配fall into a trap 落入陷阱。
▶The most successful people I know are primarily internally driven; they do what they do to impress themselves and because they feel compelled to make something happen in the world.
我认识的最成功的人主要是内在驱动的：他们做事是为了让自己满意，也因为他们感到一种使命般的冲动，要在这个世界上促成一些事。
  • 搭配feel compelled to do 感到不得不做、有强烈冲动去做。compel /kəmˈpel/ v. 强迫、驱使。
▶After you've made enough money to buy whatever you want and gotten enough social status that it stops being fun to get more, this is the only force I know of that will continue to drive you to higher levels of performance.
当你赚的钱已经足够买任何想要的东西，社会地位也高到再多一点都不再有乐趣时，这就是我所知道的唯一一种能继续推动你达到更高表现水平的力量。
  • 搭配stop doing 停止做（区分 stop to do 停下来去做）。
  • 搭配drive sb to 驱使某人达到……。
  • 词performance n. 表现、业绩；演出。
▶This is why the question of a person's motivation is so important.
这就是为什么一个人的动机问题如此重要。
  • 词motivation /ˌməʊtɪˈveɪʃn/ n. 动机、积极性。motivate v.
▶It's the first thing I try to understand about someone.
这是我了解一个人时最先想弄清楚的事。
▶The right motivations are hard to define a set of rules for, but you know it when you see it.
正确的动机很难用一套规则来定义，但你一看到就知道。
  • 搭配a set of rules 一套规则。
  • 搭配you know it when you see it 看到就知道（难以定义但一眼可辨）。
▶Jessica Livingston and Paul Graham are my benchmarks for this.
杰西卡·利文斯顿和保罗·格雷厄姆就是我在这方面的标杆。
▶YC was widely mocked for the first few years, and almost no one thought it would be a big success when they first started.
YC 在最初几年饱受嘲讽，他们刚起步时几乎没人认为它会大获成功。
  • 词mock /mɒk/ v. 嘲笑 adj. 模拟的（mock exam 模拟考）。
  • 词widely adv. 广泛地。
▶But they thought it would be great for the world if it worked, and they love helping people, and they were convinced their new model was better than the existing model.
但他们认为，如果它能成功，对世界会很有益；他们热爱帮助别人；他们也坚信自己的新模式比现有模式更好。
  • 搭配be convinced (that) 坚信……。
  • 词existing adj. 现存的、现有的。
▶Eventually, you will define your success by performing excellent work in areas that are important to you.
最终，你会以"在对你重要的领域做出卓越的工作"来定义自己的成功。
▶The sooner you can start off in that direction, the further you will be able to go.
你越早朝那个方向出发，就能走得越远。
▶It is hard to be wildly successful at anything you aren't obsessed with.
在你不痴迷的事情上，很难取得巨大的成功。
  • 词wildly adv. 极其、非常（口语强调）。
  • 搭配be obsessed with 痴迷于、着迷于。obsession n.
